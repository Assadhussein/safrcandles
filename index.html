ABOUT US

We believe fragrance can transport you anywhere—and that’s exactly what we do. Inspired by meaningful moments of South Asian heritage, our candles are designed to spark treasured memories through scent.


Each candle is thoughtfully created to bring a touch of warmth to your space. And with our unique fragrances, Safr candles is the perfect place for a thoughtful gift, for yourself or your loved ones.


Try one of our candles and see where it takes you.
