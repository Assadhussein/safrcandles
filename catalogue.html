NANI KA GHAR

A soft breeze drifts through an open window, carrying whiffs of coconut and bergamot. The gentle hum of a ceiling fan stirs the air, mingling with the aroma of blooming peonies and the sweetness of tonka, like the comforting atmosphere of a well-loved home.

Creamy sandalwood and rich vanilla anchor the fragrance, bringing back memories of polished wooden furniture that has stood the test of time. Fresh jasmine and rose come together, bringing to mind the garlands worn around hands during cherished traditions.

This is not just a fragrance; it is an ode to a place where time slows down, where love was served in small cups of chai, and every corner held the story of a life lived fully.

Nani Ka Ghar is a journey back to where it all began: comforting, feminine, and fresh.

KITAB

Step into the quiet of an old haveli library, where the air smells of worn books and teakwood shelves. The sweetness of peach and plum drifts like sunlight filtering through ornate jali windows, mingling with the delicate notes of peony and violet.

As the fragrance deepens, the warm aroma of leather and patchouli emerges. Vanilla and musk linger softly like the whispering of stories shared over dimly lit lanterns.

Kitab is a celebration of the beauty in age, the stories etched into the leather of bindings, and the quiet allure of discovery in a world where tradition and imagination meet.

BARISH

The monsoon arrives, and with it comes the intoxicating scent of mitti—the rain-soaked earth. Baarish opens with fresh, cooling notes of green florals and cucumber, capturing the relief of the first raindrops after a hot day.

The fragrance deepens to the aroma of damp soil rises and mingles with white florals and the golden warmth of amber, like scattered flower petals caught in the downpour. The base of patchouli and moss mix with sandalwood and dark musk for a thunderous finish.

Barish is a tribute to the monsoon season, where the scent of earth, rain, and blooms come alive in a moment of pure harmony.

BAZAR

Step into the vibrant charm of an old-world marketplace, where the lively air is fragrant with the aroma of hand-ground spices. The scent opens with peppercorn, cardamom, and bergamot—a burst of warmth and zest, like stepping into a narrow street bustling with vendors and brightly colored stalls.

Deeper into the market, saffron and incense fill the air. The base of patchouli, vetiver, and tobacco leaves carries the earthy scent of freshly packed herbs and woven bags, while amberwood and ambergris add a golden glow.

Bazar is a celebration of heritage—a sensory journey through timeless streets, where every scent tells a story of tradition.
